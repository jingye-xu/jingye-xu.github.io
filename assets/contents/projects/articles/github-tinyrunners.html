TinyRunners

The training-side repository for the 2022 TinyML Design Contest at ICCAD, where the team ranked 20th out of more than 150 teams.

Highlights

  • Contains data preparation utilities, model design, and PyTorch training code.
  • Stores model structure under NN/model and includes a pretrained ONNX model.
  • Documents deployment handoff to the companion STM32 C-code repository.
  • Connects to embedded health AI through ventricular arrhythmia detection on constrained hardware.
